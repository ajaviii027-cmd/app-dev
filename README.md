# My Favorite Movie: *Spider-Man: No Way Home*



## 📖 Overview  
*Spider-Man: No Way Home* is one of the best superhero movies I’ve ever watched.  
It’s about **Peter Parker**, who just wants to live a normal life after his secret identity gets exposed.  
But when he asks **Doctor Strange** for help, things go totally wrong and open the **multiverse**, bringing back old villains and even other versions of Spider-Man!

---

## Main Characters  
1. **Peter Parker / Spider-Man (Tom Holland)** – The main hero.  
2. **MJ (Zendaya)** – Peter’s girlfriend who always stays by his side.  
3. **Doctor Strange (Benedict Cumberbatch)** – The wizard who tries to help Peter fix everything.  
4. **Green Goblin, Doc Ock, Electro** – The villains.
